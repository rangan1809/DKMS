How do I listen when feedback stings?

Listening self-assessment · Receive, Reflect, Respond
This is a private reflection, not a test you pass or fail.
Answer for what you actually do when feedback is uncomfortable, not what you would like to do. Your answers stay on this device; they are not sent to DKMS or The Catalyst.

Part 1 · Your listening habits

Choose one response for each statement. Answer all 12 to calculate your section scores.

Part 2 · What your answers say

Each section has three statements and scores from 3 to 12. A higher score means you report using that listening behaviour more often.

How to use your scores
Your lowest section is a useful place to practise first. If two sections are equally low, choose the one that would make the biggest difference in your next feedback conversation. There are no pass/fail bands or diagnostic labels.

Part 3 · Biases that can get in the way

Tick one or two you recognise in yourself when feedback stings.

You can select up to two.
